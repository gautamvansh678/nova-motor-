NOVA MOTORS — CAR SHOWROOM WEBSITE
Files: index.html, style.css, script.js
RUN: Open this folder in VS Code and launch index.html using Live Server, or open index.html in your browser.
CUSTOMIZE:
1. In script.js, replace WHATSAPP_NUMBER with your WhatsApp number (country code + number, digits only).
2. Update showroom name, contact details, inventory, vehicle specifications and prices in index.html.
3. Replace sample car photos with your own showroom/vehicle photos if desired.
NOTE: Cars and prices are placeholders. Enquiry buttons open WhatsApp. No inventory database, finance application, or backend is connected. Internet is required for online fonts/images.
